Privacy

Privacy policy

The short version: this website has no tracking, no advertising and no cookies. We only see what you choose to send us, and we use it to reply to you and build your website.

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Who we are

This website belongs to laylatul-code, a web studio. If you have any question about your information, email us at abus76416@gmail.com.

What we collect

When you use the request form, you can give us your name, business name, phone or WhatsApp number, city, the kind of website and features you want, your budget, your timing, and any notes. The form doesn't send anything to us by itself: it writes your request into a message and opens WhatsApp or your email app. We only receive it if you press send there. When you send it, we also see your WhatsApp number or email address.

If you're our client, your project page holds your project's details, progress and preview link, and the messages, tickets and comments you and we write there.

If you use the "Ask us" assistant, the questions you type are sent to an AI service to write the answers. Please don't type personal or sensitive information into it.

We don't use analytics, advertising trackers or cookies. Your project page remembers your access code only until you close that browser tab.

How we use it

  • To reply to you and answer your questions.
  • To prepare your price and launch date.
  • If you become a client, to build, launch and support your website, and to keep the records of our work together.

We don't sell your information, and we don't use it to market other people's products to you.

Who else handles it

  • WhatsApp or your email provider carries your message to us when you send it, under their own privacy policies.
  • Our hosting (our own server, and GitHub Pages for a demo copy) delivers this website and may record technical details, such as your IP address, to deliver the pages and keep them secure.
  • Google Fonts supplies the website's fonts, so your browser connects to Google to download them.
  • Supabase stores client project pages, messages, tickets and comments, and runs the assistant.
  • Google Gemini writes the assistant's answers from the questions you type. Google handles them under its own terms, which for free use can include using them to improve its services.

How long we keep it

We keep your messages for as long as we need them to reply to you and, if you become a client, for the length of your project and any period the law requires us to keep records.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to show you the information we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it. Email abus76416@gmail.com and we'll reply within one working day.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle your information, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
