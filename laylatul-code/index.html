Questions

Asked before every project.

How long will my website take?

A Starter site takes about 7 days, a Business site about 2 to 3 weeks, and a store or web app about 3 to 6 weeks. Your exact date comes with your quote, in writing.

Who owns the website when it is done?

You do. The domain and hosting are registered in your name, and you get every login and every file. If you ever want to move, you take it all with you. No buyout fee.

Are there any hidden costs?

No. Your price is fixed before we start. The only other cost is your domain and hosting, which you pay directly, in your name. We tell you the exact amount up front.

What if I want changes after launch?

Small edits are free for 30 days after launch. After that, message us and we tell you the price of a change before we make it.

I don't have photos or text. Can you help?

Yes. We write the words with you and find or create the pictures. You approve everything before it goes live.

Will it work on phones?

Yes. Most of your visitors will be on a phone, so we design for phones first and test on real ones before launch.

How do I pay?

Half to start and half when you are happy, before the site goes live. UPI and bank transfer both work.

What if you stop replying?

We reply within one working day, and you have a preview link to see progress at any time. You never have to wonder what is happening.
