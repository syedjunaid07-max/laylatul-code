# laylatul-code: Design Package (Tier 1)

Lives outside the deploy folder. Every line of copy here ships verbatim.

## 1. Brand premise

"Laylat" means night. Every website starts as an idea in the dark: a spark only the owner can see. laylatul-code gathers that spark into a working, glowing website. The whole site teaches one idea: **you bring the idea, we build the light around it, and it is yours to keep.** The hero is light gathering into a site. The request builder lets the visitor watch their own site gather from light as they choose. The close is ownership.

## 6. Below-fold outline

1. **Heard this before?** Quotes in buyers' words get struck through on scroll, then "Not here."
   - "My designer stopped replying after I paid the deposit."
   - "It was meant to take two weeks. It has been four months."
   - "The price kept going up halfway through."
   - "They registered my domain in their name and want money to hand it over."
   - "I can't even change my phone number on my own website."
2. **Four promises** (sticky title left, list right): Fixed price before we start. A date, in writing. Everything in your name. A reply within one working day.
3. **Any website. Really.** Ten type tiles; click one to start a request with it picked.
4. **How it works**: Tell us. Get your price and date. Watch it being built. Go live and own it. Self-drawing line.
5. **Prices**: Starter from Rs 9,999 / Business from Rs 24,999 / Store and Custom from Rs 49,999.
6. **Build your request** (the signature interactive moment): choices on the left, a live blueprint of their site gathers from light on the right. Sends to WhatsApp (prefilled message) or email (prefilled). Success state tells the truth: the message opens in their app and they press send.
7. **FAQ**: timing, ownership, hidden costs, changes after launch, no photos or text, phones, payment, going quiet.
8. Final call + footer (email, WhatsApp). No fictional disclosure (real business). AI imagery to be swapped for real work later.
